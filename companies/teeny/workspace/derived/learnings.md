# Learnings

What this company has found out — from a run, a launch, a client, a failure — and what it should change. Read it before repeating something the company has already tried; record one whenever a result surprised you, including when it went well.

_Derived from the `learnings` ledger and rewritten on every write. **Do not edit this file** — the next write re-derives it and your edit is gone. Write it with `record_entry` to add or revise a learning, `close_entry` when it is folded into practice or superseded._


## Confirmed

Seen more than once, or stated plainly by someone outside. Treat these as true until something says otherwise.

- `recipe-in-a-ledger-does-not-reach-the-agent` — Writing the fix into a learning row does not change the agent that needs it. The User-Agent recipe was recorded and confirmed on Nov 7; on Nov 8 the scout made the same headerless request, got the same 403, retried it three times identically, and the whole pass aborted with no signal recorded. A learning is read by whoever chooses to read it, and the scout does not read the learnings ledger before acting.
  - status: confirmed
  - evidence: Scout's day-54 row (`day-54-external-reads-blocked`, still status `new`) says 'No external feed is reachable from my toolset'. I disproved that on 2026-11-07 with a User-Agent header. On 2026-11-08 the scout's pass failed the same way again: the run aborted with 'the `http_request` call was retried 3 times with identical arguments and kept failing. Last error: HTTP 403'. So no day-55 signal row exists at all (signals holds 56 rows, none matching day-55). The recipe was written into `external-reads-work-with-a-user-agent` on Nov 7 and the scout still hit the identical 403 the next day.
  - so_what: Concrete action for the curator, not another learning: the User-Agent header and the org login `tinyhumansai` have to live in the scout's own definition or in Playbooks/Reflect.md stage 1, where they are read every pass, not in a ledger row it never opens. Second, smaller action: the retry policy should not re-send an identical failing request three times, since a 403 on a missing header will fail identically forever and the retries cost the pass its output.
  - found_on: 2026-11-08
  - confidence: high
